Local-first, explained

Your customers.
Your device.

Community Edition keeps customer records in the browser where you use it. It does not automatically upload those records to De-Omega-Point.

Local storage needs care: clearing browser data or losing the device can remove records. Export regular backups. Automatic multi-device synchronisation is not included.

Community & pilot details

A few useful answers.

Who is this for?

Independent cafés, shops, salons and service businesses looking for a straightforward customer loyalty workflow.

Do I need to register interest to explore the app?

No. The pilot-interest form is optional. Community Edition has a separate registration step inside the app.

Can I pay or download a native app here?

No. Online payments and Android/iOS downloads are unavailable here. The app access page links to the existing web edition and explains installation options.

Will it guarantee more sales?

No sales outcome is guaranteed. Start with a small trial and track repeat visits, reward use and time spent running the programme.
