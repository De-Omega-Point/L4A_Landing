Privacy & data · 15 October 2026

Know where your information goes.

This notice covers the Loyalty-4-All product website and optional pilot requests. The Community Edition app has a separate privacy notice.

Browsing this website

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, account cookies or payment processors. Its hosting provider may process standard connection and security logs when you visit.

The form does not save your personal details to this site’s localStorage or send them to a registration server. Details stay in the page while you review them. Your browser may retain form or page state; use “Clear this request” when sharing a device.

Optional pilot requests

The form asks for a business name and contact email; your name and business type are optional. It prepares a message to hello@de-omega-point.com. De-Omega-Point receives it only if you send it from your email service.

We use a request to respond about the pilot and provide requested support. Occasional product updates and AI marketing or industry news require the separate, unchecked opt-in. Declining updates does not block a pilot request. No automated update feed is promised.

Email copies are held by your provider and the receiving email provider. Copying a request may place it on your clipboard; saving it creates a text file you control. Do not include passwords, payment details or your customers’ information.

Ask us by email to access, correct or delete request information, or to stop optional updates. We keep correspondence while needed to handle the request and any necessary records. Provider backups and obligations may affect deletion timing.

Community Edition customer records

The app stores customer, visit, points and reward records locally in the browser used for the app. It does not automatically upload those customer records to De-Omega-Point. Core loyalty use does not require registration. Optional Community Intelligence registration shares the business/contact details, selected interests and consent record with the app’s membership service. It does not include local customer records.

Local storage is not a backup. Clearing browser data, switching browsers or losing a device may remove access to records. Export backups and protect the device and backup files. The app does not automatically synchronise records across devices.

Payments & old prototype data

No payment is taken here. This website does not issue paid entitlements, access serials or secure download links. Browser data and URL parameters cannot confirm payment.
