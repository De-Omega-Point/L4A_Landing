Privacy promise

Simple, clear access records.

Loyalty4All keeps payment and access handling separated so customers know what is stored and why.

Payments

PayPal handles payment processing. Customers complete payment through PayPal Checkout, and this frontend does not store card numbers, CVV codes or bank details.

Access details

Only access details needed to provide the app are stored: customer email, selected plan, payment status, serial number, access status and app version.

Serial numbers

Serial numbers control app access and help support match a customer to the right plan. A serial number may be suspended or reissued when access changes.

No selling user data

User data is not sold. Information is used to provide access, support customers and maintain accurate payment and subscription records.
